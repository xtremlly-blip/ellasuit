The House of ELLA

Curating World-Class Menswear Brands for the Modern Gentleman

At ELLA Suit Store, we believe every distinguished gentleman deserves access to world-renowned sartorial elegance. Rather than bespoke tailoring, we specialize as experienced suppliers of high-quality, pre-tailored, ready-to-wear luxury suits, fine shirts, and handcrafted leather footwear imported from global fashion capitals—including Italy, Turkey, the UK, Europe, and the US.

Each garment in our Addis Ababa boutique is hand-selected for its superior drape, rich fabric compositions (Super 150s virgin wool, Egyptian cotton, calfskin leather), and impeccable international sizing standards.

Client Assistance

Frequently Asked Questions

Our entire collection consists of premium, imported ready-to-wear garments carefully sourced from top international brands in Italy, Turkey, Europe, and the US. They are pre-cut to international standard European/US sizing, offering immediate elegance without waiting weeks for custom tailoring.
Our main boutique is conveniently located along Bole Road in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. You are welcome to visit our showroom to try on our imported collections or consult with our style advisors in person.
While our suits are pre-tailored to high-end European standard cuts, minor adjustments (such as trouser hem length or sleeve tweaking) can be easily handled by master tailors locally in Addis Ababa to achieve your ideal silhouette.
You can send us an inquiry directly through WhatsApp or Telegram using the buttons on this website. Simply share the item you are interested in along with your size, and our team will confirm availability and delivery options immediately.
