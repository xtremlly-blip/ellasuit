The House of ELLA

Curating World-Class Menswear Brands for the Modern Gentleman

At ELLA Suit Store, we believe every distinguished gentleman deserves access to world-renowned sartorial elegance. Rather than bespoke tailoring, we specialize as experienced suppliers of high-quality, pre-tailored, ready-to-wear luxury suits, fine shirts, and handcrafted leather footwear imported from global fashion capitals—including Italy, Turkey, the UK, Europe, and the US.

Each garment in our Addis Ababa boutique is hand-selected for its superior drape, rich fabric compositions (Super 150s virgin wool, Egyptian cotton, calfskin leather), and impeccable international sizing standards.
